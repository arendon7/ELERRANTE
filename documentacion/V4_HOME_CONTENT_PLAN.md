# EL ERRANTE · Home V4 Content Plan

## Visual thesis

A dark editorial food world in motion: deep carbon black, ivory, restrained aged gold, cinematic food/process imagery and a walking pizzaiolo emblem that makes movement part of the brand rather than decoration.

## Interaction thesis

Motion is sparse and purposeful:

1. hero copy/emblem reveal with a short stagger;
2. selected image crops respond subtly to scroll/hover without hijacking reading;
3. section transitions use opacity/translate and line reveals, with reduced-motion fallback.

## Conversion thesis

The Home has two primary jobs:

1. create desire and recognition for El Errante;
2. route visitors quickly to `Tienda / En Casa` or `Eventos`.

Method, Bitácora and author content deepen trust after desire is established.

## Section sequence

### 01 Hero · Brand world

**Job:** recognition + desire + first action.

Required:

- approved V4 master logo/emblem;
- one cinematic hero image with strong negative space;
- one short headline;
- one short supporting line;
- primary CTA: pizzas / En Casa;
- secondary CTA: events / movement.

No essay in the first viewport.

### 02 Manifesto · The point of view

**Job:** explain what makes El Errante different in one idea.

Use one large editorial sentence and one process/matter image.

Core territory:

> Aprendida viajando. Interpretada desde Colombia.

or equivalent validated copy.

### 03 La carta · Five expressions

**Job:** product desire + differentiation.

Do not render five equal cards.

Use one dominant pizza at a time or an asymmetric editorial rail/composition. Each pizza receives:

- name;
- territory;
- one sentence;
- product image;
- direct link.

### 04 En Casa · Segundo Fuego

**Job:** make the commercial proposition immediately understandable.

Surface copy should explain the two-fire idea without burying the customer in process detail.

Visual sequence should show transformation/ritual rather than a generic frozen-product packshot only.

### 05 En Movimiento · Hospitality

**Job:** sell events and mobility.

Need distinct assets for:

- arrival/context;
- operation/fire;
- service/hospitality.

Do not reuse the Home hero as an event-operation substitute.

### 06 Despensa · Objects

**Job:** expose sauces, reductions, flour/other validated items as authored culinary objects.

Prefer still-life/object photography and restrained product metadata. Avoid e-commerce card wall aesthetic.

### 07 Método + Bitácora · Evidence

**Job:** prove there is thinking behind the product.

Use only 2–3 evidence fragments. Home should tease depth; dedicated pages contain the full argument.

### 08 Juan David · Authorship

**Job:** give the work a responsible human author.

Portrait/action image in real culinary context preferred over a corporate profile treatment.

Copy should focus on way of seeing/cooking rather than CV density.

### 09 Closing frame

**Job:** memorable final action.

One strong image or black field, one statement, one CTA pair.

## Home copy density budget

Target visible copy reduction versus V3 Home:

- hero supporting copy: ~20–35 words;
- manifesto: ~15–35 words plus optional short paragraph;
- product item: ~12–25 words each;
- En Casa: ~40–70 words on surface;
- En Movimiento: ~35–60 words on surface;
- Method/Bitácora: ~20–40 words each;
- Author: ~40–70 words.

Long explanations are moved to dedicated pages, not deleted from the content system.

## UX rules

- sticky/header navigation must not overpower hero;
- logo remains legible at small widths;
- primary CTA contrast must be obvious;
- no hidden horizontal overflow on product exploration;
- all interactive rails/carousels require keyboard-accessible alternatives;
- no autoplay motion essential to comprehension;
- image aspect ratios must be explicit;
- mobile first viewport prioritizes logo/name, headline, image and one primary CTA.
