# Gold use V4

Gold is a signal, not a surface treatment.

Good uses:

- thin rules;
- small captions/numbers;
- selected hover/focus emphasis;
- restrained emblem details where part of master artwork.

Avoid:

- gold gradients across large areas;
- metallic glow;
- gold body copy;
- making every button/border gold;
- yellow/orange photography grading to fake luxury.
