# V4 Design Review Prompt

When reviewing a public page, ask:

1. Is this unmistakably El Errante without relying on black/gold alone?
2. Does product/food create desire before explanation begins?
3. Is the page editorial rather than templated?
4. Does every major image have a unique narrative role?
5. Is gold restrained and food color natural?
6. Is copy shorter on the surface without losing truthful depth?
7. Does movement feel purposeful?
8. Is mobile independently composed?
9. Are commerce/navigation actions obvious?
10. Does it pass accessibility, performance and regression gates?
