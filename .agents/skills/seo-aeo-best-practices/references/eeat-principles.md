# EEAT reference

Adapted from `sanity-io/agent-toolkit` (reviewed 2026-09-02).

For El Errante, experience/expertise/trust should be demonstrated through real culinary work rather than generic authority claims.

## Signals worth preserving

- named authorship where relevant;
- real process, tests, observations and case evidence;
- publication/update dates for editorial material;
- primary sources when technical claims depend on them;
- clear contact/help routes;
- transparent commercial state;
- privacy/terms/trust information;
- HTTPS and no misleading claims.

`Método`, `Bitácora`, `Historia` and `Juan David` are natural EEAT surfaces. Do not turn Home into a credentials page; V4's narrative order remains binding: work first, author later, method underneath, evidence always.
