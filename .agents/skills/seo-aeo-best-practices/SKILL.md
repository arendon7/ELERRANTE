# SEO & AEO Best Practices · El Errante

Apply this skill when implementing or reviewing:

- page metadata and Open Graph;
- canonical URLs;
- `robots.txt` and XML sitemaps;
- JSON-LD structured data;
- crawl/index boundaries between the public site and internal/admin surfaces;
- author/evidence signals in Método, Bitácora and Juan David;
- content structure for search engines and AI answer surfaces.

## Project-specific rules

1. Brand/product truth remains authoritative. Never invent address, ratings, reviews, availability, credentials, prices, social profiles or commercial claims for structured data.
2. Only public customer-facing pages belong in the sitemap. Internal/admin/financial/operational pages must not be promoted as public search destinations.
3. Canonical URLs must point to the GitHub Pages production origin until an approved custom domain replaces it.
4. Structured data must reflect visible or otherwise verifiable page content.
5. SEO must not damage V4 editorial hierarchy or create keyword-stuffed copy.
6. AEO is a structure/trust discipline, not a promise of inclusion in AI answers.
7. Treat crawler policy as an explicit product decision; do not silently block or enable AI-specific crawlers beyond the general public policy.
