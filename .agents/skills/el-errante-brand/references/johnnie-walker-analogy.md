# Strategic reference: movement / heritage

The Johnnie Walker reference is conceptual only.

Transfer these principles:

- a recognizable character/emblem in motion;
- continuity between heritage and contemporary expression;
- product-first cinematic storytelling;
- restrained but recognizable accent color;
- ritual and progression;
- short confident messaging.

Do not copy distinctive layouts, diagonals, bottle/label devices, typographic treatments, packaging cues or trade dress.
